GPIO26  Relay module input; firmware keeps relay OFF by default

POWER NOTES
- Use the 12V adapter only for the isolated low-voltage test circuit.
- Adjust LM2596 output and verify with a multimeter before connecting 5V loads.
- ESP32 GPIO/ADC pins are 3.3V only and are NOT 5V tolerant.
- Verify actual sensor output voltages and divider ratios before attaching to ESP32.
- Use a correctly rated fuse for the adapter, wiring and test load; do not assume a 32A fuse is suitable.
- Do not connect to 230V AC mains. Do not physically short the supply; use software simulation.
- Relay is not certified protection. One current sensor reads total load current, not exact branch location.
